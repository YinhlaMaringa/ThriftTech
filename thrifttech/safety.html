About Us

We began with a simple idea: help a small clothing brand go online. As developers, we built a platform for them — and then realized this could help many more. Thrift Tech has grown into a community-driven marketplace where sellers and buyers connect over sustainable fashion.

Our platform lets independent sellers list secondhand clothing and reach a wider audience. Every listing by a user is reviewed by our manager before appearing live, ensuring quality and trust.
